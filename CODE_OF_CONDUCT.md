# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Everyone who takes part in this project, in issues, pull requests, reviews and discussions, is expected to:

- be respectful and assume good intent
- give and accept technical criticism without making it personal
- keep discussion on the work
- respect the privacy of others and never publish private information

Harassment, insults, discriminatory language, sexualised content and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Defensive use

The tools in this workspace are defensive. Do not use project spaces to request, share or coordinate unauthorised access to systems or data.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour with the "Report content" action that GitHub provides on every issue, pull request and comment, or contact the repository owner through their GitHub profile. Reports are handled confidentially.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that break this code, and may temporarily or permanently restrict a participant. Decisions are explained to the people involved.
